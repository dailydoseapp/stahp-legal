Stahp Privacy Policy Last updated: November 2026 · Contact: dailydosetanks@gmail.com Stahp ("the app") helps you manage screen-time limits, lockdown warnings, and optional premium credits. This policy describes what data we handle and how. 1. Data stored on your device Most app data stays on your phone in a local database (Room), including: Screen-time session counters and limit settings Lockdown and demo-mode state Available credits and subscription tier cached locally Optional activity logs (lockdown events) This local database is protected by your device's operating-system security. It is not encrypted with a separate app-specific key unless your device encrypts storage at rest. 2. Account authentication If you sign in, we use Firebase Authentication (email / Google Sign-In) to verify your account. Firebase processes your email address according to Google's policies. If Firebase is not configured, the app may use a limited offline account stored only on your device. 3. Subscriptions and payments Premium plans are billed through Google Play Billing. Payment details are handled by Google Play, not stored in the app. The app may cache your subscription tier, auto-renew status, and credit balance locally (and optionally sync credit state with our servers if configured). You can cancel auto-renewal from the app Profile tab (opens Google Play subscriptions) or in the Google Play Store. 4. What we do not do We do not sell your personal data. We do not use lockdown logs for advertising. We do not transmit routine screen-time metrics to our servers unless you use cloud sign-in or optional sync features described above. 5. Permissions The app may request: Notifications — lockdown and tracking alerts Foreground service — enforce session limits while protection is active Full-screen intent — lockdown alarm overlay when a limit is breached Internet — sign-in, subscription sync, and support features Vibration / wake lock — alarm feedback during lockdown 6. Account and data deletion You can delete your account from the app (Profile → Legal & Support → Delete Account). This removes local app data and attempts to remove your Firebase sign-in. For leftover cloud or billing follow-up, email dailydosetanks@gmail.com from your account address. Manage or cancel Google Play subscriptions in Google Play. 7. Children The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. 8. Changes We may update this policy. The in-app copy and this page will be revised when practices change materially. Terms of Service
