Stahp Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026 · Contact: dailydosetanks@gmail.com

Stahp ("the app") helps you manage screen-time limits, lockdown warnings, and optional premium credits. This policy describes what data we handle and how.

1. Data stored on your device

Most app data stays on your phone in a local database (Room), including:

  • Screen-time session counters and limit settings
  • Lockdown and demo-mode state
  • Available credits and subscription tier cached locally
  • Optional activity logs (lockdown events)

This local database is protected by your device's operating-system security. It is not encrypted with a separate app-specific key unless your device encrypts storage at rest.

2. Account authentication

If you sign in, we use Firebase Authentication (Google) to verify your email and manage your account. Firebase processes your email address according to Google's policies. If Firebase is not configured, the app may use a limited offline account stored only on your device.

3. Subscriptions and payments

Premium plans are processed by Stripe. Payment card details are handled by Stripe, not stored in the app. Our billing server may store your email and subscription status to sync your plan and daily credits. You can cancel auto-renewal from the app Profile tab or by contacting support.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not use lockdown logs for advertising.
  • We do not transmit routine screen-time metrics to our servers unless you use cloud sign-in or billing features described above.

5. Permissions

The app may request:

  • Notifications — lockdown and tracking alerts
  • Foreground service — enforce session limits while protection is active
  • Internet — sign-in, subscription sync, and billing
  • Vibration / wake lock — alarm feedback during lockdown

6. Account and data deletion

You can delete your account from the app (Profile → Legal & Support → Delete Account). This removes local app data and attempts to remove your Firebase sign-in. For billing records held by Stripe or support follow-up, email dailydosetanks@gmail.com from your account address.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The in-app copy and this page will be revised when practices change materially.
